# Original figure image provenance and limited use

These three images are the original **Figure 1**, **Figure 2**, and **Figure 3**
from Alexander's *Are Chads more misogynistic?* (2023), obtained from the
copy of the article used for the review:
https://datepsychology.com/are-chads-more-misogynistic/

They are included only so the accompanying figure-label discrepancy can be
re-examined and the three-panel critical comparison reproduced. All rights
in those original third-party figures remain with their respective owner(s).
